# Paper Claims Matrix

Status labels:

- Verified: supported by local code/log/table evidence, with stated scope.
- Weak Support: some evidence exists, but scope, consistency, or provenance is insufficient.
- Unsupported: no adequate repository evidence found.
- Overstated: evidence exists but the manuscript claim is broader or stronger than the evidence.
- Unclear: local evidence is internally inconsistent or not decision-safe.

## Defensible Innovation Reframing

Current evidence supports this safer contribution angle:

> A delayed-feedback online load-forecasting framework that combines factorized expert forecasting, invariant/variant representation diagnostics, and conservative stage-wise adaptation. The strongest current evidence is improvement over frozen deployment on shifted public benchmarks, portability across tested transformer backbones, and practical tradeoffs between lightweight transient correction and fuller online controllers. The method is competitive rather than uniformly superior to prior online adaptation baselines, especially on Lishui-9.

Do not claim:

- Universal SOTA.
- Large or statistically significant superiority.
- VAE gate as a proven core innovation.
- Stage II or Full controller superiority until leakage/provenance issues are resolved.
- Faithful reproduction of external online baselines without additional evidence.
