# Risk Register

Severity scale:

- Critical: can invalidate central experimental claims or require relabeling results.
- High: likely to cause rejection or major revision if not fixed before submission.
- Medium: weakens reproducibility, clarity, or reviewer confidence.
- Low: polish/format issue after scientific risks are controlled.
